## Comparison with HistGradientBoosting

The closest reference is the O01 tuned HistGradientBoosting validation result:

| Feature set | Tuned HGB average precision | LightGBM average precision | Difference (LightGBM − HGB) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Full | 0.935259 | 0.935489 | +0.000230 |
| Conservative | 0.786295 | 0.789088 | +0.002793 |

The corresponding untuned HGB baselines were 0.934302 (full) and 0.785706 (conservative). LightGBM is the strongest G01 result on both feature sets by average precision, but its margin over tuned HGB is small. These are single frozen validation-split comparisons; no confidence interval or statistical significance claim is available.

## Interpretation

1. **Full feature set:** LightGBM has the highest average precision (0.935489), narrowly above tuned HGB (0.935259). CatBoost is lower at 0.931280. At threshold 0.5, CatBoost and LightGBM trade precision for recall relative to the tuned HGB reference: LightGBM recall is 0.8940 and precision 0.7585, while tuned HGB recall is 0.7807 and precision 0.8994. Threshold-dependent decisions should therefore not be ranked by average precision alone.
2. **Conservative feature set:** LightGBM reaches average precision 0.789088, only 0.002793 above tuned HGB. CatBoost reaches 0.781258. The conservative benchmark remains materially weaker than the full-feature benchmark.
3. **Runtime:** In this run, LightGBM completed in about 1.1–1.3 seconds per feature set, versus about 4.7–4.9 seconds for CatBoost. Runtime is environment- and run-dependent.
4. **Model choice:** LightGBM is the current validation leader for the next research stage, not a proven universally superior model. Retain tuned HGB as a close reference and CatBoost as a secondary comparison.

## Reproducibility and isolation

- Fixed split and seed 42.
- Fixed model configurations; no validation-driven tuning or early stopping.
- Training partition used for fitting; validation partition used for the reported evaluation.
- Test partition used: **False**.
- Local outputs: `experiments/results/boosting/validation_metrics.csv`, `run_metadata.json`, and four fitted pipelines.
- Results are run-specific and should be retained with the metadata generated by the experiment.

## Limitations

The dataset is a synthetic/augmented benchmark with unresolved feature provenance and feature-timing questions. These metrics do not establish real-world creditworthiness, repayment/default prediction, causal effects, deployment readiness, or fairness. A small validation improvement does not demonstrate statistical significance or external generalization.
